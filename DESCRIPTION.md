This mod makes doors spooky, imitating the way that doors behave in horror games like Amnesia or Outlast. This means you can peek through doors by opening them halfway, slam them shut if you get scared, and enjoy an orchestra of creaking sounds while you do so.

This mod requires [Balm](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/balm), which must be installed alongside the mod.

This mod was made for [SpookyJam 2024](https://spooky-jam.com/events/2024/).

Note that only 1.20.1, 1.21.1 and 26.1+ are supported. In older versions, spooky doors are limited to separate Spooky Door items instead of affecting all doors.

### ![Features](https://mods.twelveiterations.com/img/features-header.png)

*   **New:** Now affects all doors! Use Honeycomb or Ghast Tear to toggle.
*   Peek doors by dragging your mouse to open them slowly
*   Slam doors shut or open by dragging your mouse quickly
*   Run into doors that are ajar to open or close them
*   Scary door sounds when doing so
